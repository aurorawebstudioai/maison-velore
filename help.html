FAQ

How long does a bottle last?
A 100 ml bottle typically lasts 6–12 months with regular use.
What is the difference between sizes?
The fragrance is identical. 10 ml is a travel spray, 50 ml suits daily use, 100 ml is best value.
How should I store my perfume?
Cool, dry, out of sunlight, with the cap on.
