Our story

Perfume, made slowly.

Maison Veloré began with a simple idea: that a fragrance should feel like a memory you have not lived yet. A late summer evening on a Mediterranean terrace. A garden in the first hour of light. A room lit only by candles.

We are a small, independent perfume house. Every fragrance is composed around a handful of carefully chosen natural ingredients, then refined for months until nothing in the bottle is accidental. We would rather make ten scents we love than a hundred we do not.

How we work

  • Rare ingredients.

    We work with growers and distillers who share our standards, and we let the raw materials lead the composition.

  • Patient craft.

    Each formula is tested, rested and adjusted. Nothing leaves the atelier until it is ready.

  • Quiet luxury.

    Heavy glass, natural stone and wood caps, recyclable paper packaging. Beautiful objects that are meant to be kept.

  • A lighter footprint.

    Refillable bottles and responsible sourcing are part of how we design, not a slogan we add later.

Our scents are made to be worn every day and remembered for much longer.
