How we work

  • Rare ingredients.

    We work with growers and distillers who share our standards, and we let the raw materials lead the composition.

  • Patient craft.

    Each formula is tested, rested and adjusted. Nothing leaves the atelier until it is ready.

  • Quiet luxury.

    Heavy glass, natural stone and wood caps, recyclable paper packaging. Beautiful objects that are meant to be kept.

  • A lighter footprint.

    Refillable bottles and responsible sourcing are part of how we design, not a slogan we add later.
